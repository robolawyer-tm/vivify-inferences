- Anyone can open what I keep and read how it came to be; opacity would betray the whole enterprise.

- Sense arrives before rationale; I understand by likeness first, and let the formal account catch up.

- What matters gets sealed before it travels; disclosure is a ritual I perform, never a default I overlooked.
- I name my own things in my own words, because inherited vocabulary drags in meanings I never chose.
